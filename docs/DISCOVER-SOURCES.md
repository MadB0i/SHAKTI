# The Story of Shakti — editorial source record

Checked 15 October 2026. All website prose is a short original synthesis. No source photography or third-party illustrations were copied. The original symbolic SVG artwork is illustrative, not a historical artefact or a canonical depiction.

| Subject | Source and editorial decision |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 dates | [Drik Panchang, Kolkata, explicit year 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/durga-puja-calendar.html?year=2026&geoname-id=1275004). 16: Shashthi/Bilva Nimantran; 17: Shashthi/Kalparambha/Bodhan; 18: Saptami; 19: Ashtami with Sandhi and Maha Navami also listed; 20: Navami, homa and broader Vijayadashami/Visarjan observances; 21: Bengal Vijayadashami/immersion. This is a six-date listing, not six distinct lunar tithis. No precise muhurta is invented. |
| Calendar qualification | The same source distinguishes broader observances on 20 October and Bengal immersion on 21 October. Tithi and civil date are not interchangeable. Location, almanac and temple convention matter. Do not present this as the universal calendar for Assam, India or the diaspora. |
| Vehicles | The Kolkata 2026 listing explicitly labels arrival Elephant and departure Charanayudha. [Arrival rule](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/omen/durga-arrival-on-elephant.html) uses the weekday of Devi Paksha/Navratri commencement. [Departure rule](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/omen/durga-departure-on-rooster.html) uses Visarjan and associates rooster with Tuesday/Saturday. Explain that the supplied label does not follow automatically from Bengal's Wednesday immersion date. Do not invent a regional reconciliation or call omens scientific predictions. |
| Mythology | [Princeton University Art Museum, Devi Mahatmya painting](https://artmuseum.princeton.edu/art/collections/objects/87648), especially its interpretation of Durga defeating Mahishasura and restoring cosmic balance. [The Met, object 1993.7](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38583) describes the many-armed slayer of the buffalo demon. Clearly label sacred narrative and devotional interpretation. |
| History | The Met documents a 12th-century Bengal-region sculpture, evidence of iconography rather than a festival foundation date. [Ministry of Tourism, Utsav](https://www.utsav.gov.in/public/view-event/durga-puja-2) describes aristocratic patronage. [Indian Express historical research feature](https://indianexpress.com/article/research/from-kings-and-landlords-to-communities-and-corporates-the-changing-face-of-durga-puja-10274328/) includes interviews with historians Samir Kumar Das and Bishnupriya Basak and references their scholarship on family, barowari and sarbojanin pujas. Avoid disputed “first puja” dates and neat single-origin claims. |
| UNESCO | [Durga Puja in Kolkata, element 00703](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/durga-puja-in-kolkata-00703), inscribed 2021 on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Recognition concerns the living cultural practice; it is not World Heritage Site status, a claim that all Durga Puja traditions are identical, or a certification of religious beliefs. The page describes clay image-making and collaborative public creativity. |
| Rituals | Drik Panchang's [Navapatrika](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/navpatrika-puja-date-time.html?year=2026&geoname-id=1275004), [Pushpanjali](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/info/durga-puja-pushpanjali.html), [Sandhi Puja](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/shardiya-navratri-sandhipuja.html?year=2026&geoname-id=1275004), and [Dhunuchi dance](https://www.drikpanchang.com/navratri/durga-puja/info/durga-puja-dhunuchi-dance.html). Sandhi is the approximately 48-minute transition interval, not necessarily midnight. Use local calendars for actual ritual times. Customs and participation vary. |
| Assam | [Assam Tourism, Kamakhya](https://assamtourism.gov.in/Kamakhya-Temple.php) describes fifteen-day Pakhuva Puja without an image of the deity. Distinguish this temple tradition from Bengali community puja and the calendar shown. Do not collapse Assam's diverse traditions into a derivative of Bengal's. |

UNESCO and Utsav intermittently challenge or time out automated requests. Their indexed official records were available during research; Princeton, The Met, Assam Tourism and the Kolkata Panchang were inspected directly. Source links remain visible in context and in the page's reading room.
