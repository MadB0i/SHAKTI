## LinkedIn

I built SHAKTI — The Awakening as a small interactive film for Durga Puja.

It begins with a diya you can ignite. The first 16 seconds run in WebGL, using Three.js and custom shaders; the finale combines AI-assisted footage with code-rendered typography. An original synthesized score carries the 30-second journey across the two formats.

The most interesting work was in the transitions: keeping the camera, light, sound and portrait framing coherent while making pause, seeking and replay reliable.

## Reddit / developer communities

Title: I built a 30-second interactive Durga Puja cinematic with Three.js

SHAKTI starts with one diya you can ignite, then follows a Trishul through a cosmic gateway to Maa Durga. The opening 16 seconds are real-time WebGL; the finale uses AI-assisted footage. The titles and Bengali greeting are rendered in code, and the new score is synthesized without samples.

I worked on matching the two visual formats, composing for both 9:16 and desktop, and keeping pause/replay/seeking consistent across the boundary. React, TypeScript, Three.js, GLSL, GSAP and Vite are the main tools.

Code and preview: https://github.com/MadB0i/SHAKTI
