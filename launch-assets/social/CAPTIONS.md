## Reddit — creative coding

**Title:** I built an interactive Durga Puja film that begins with one diya

SHAKTI starts with a diya you can ignite, then follows a 3D Trishul through a cosmic gateway to Maa Durga.

The first 16 seconds are real-time WebGL; the finale uses AI-assisted footage. The Bengali/English greetings are rendered in code. I used React, Three.js, GLSL and GSAP, and spent most of the time on the transition between the two formats and making seeking/replay behave consistently.

Live: https://madb0i.github.io/SHAKTI/
Code: https://github.com/MadB0i/SHAKTI

I'd be interested in feedback on the transition and how the dark scenes read on a phone.
